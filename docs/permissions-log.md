# Permissions log

Who the site names or shows, and the permission behind it. The owner confirmed on 2026-11-13 that **written permission is on file** for every client and consultant below. This page records it so the claim can be checked later.

A test (`src/content/permissions-log.test.ts`) fails when the site names a client or a consultant that is not listed here. Add the row first, then the content.

How to fill a row:

- **Shown** is what the site publishes: the company name, a quote, a result, a logo, a person's name, their role, their photo.
- **Permission** is the form: signed release, email, or contract clause.
- **Date** is when it was given. **Kept at** is where the document is stored (a folder, a ticket, a mailbox label). Do not paste the document here.
- If someone withdraws permission, remove them from the site first, then mark the row withdrawn with the date.
